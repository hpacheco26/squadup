# Invite Flows

SquadUp has two distinct invite mechanisms.

## 1. Group invite codes
- 8-char alphanumeric (alphabet excludes `0 1 I O l`).
- Generated by `inviteService.createInvite(group, uid)` and stored in `invites/{code}`.
- Shared as `https://squadupv2.web.app/join/{CODE}`.
- Recipient lands on [JoinPage.jsx](../src/pages/JoinPage.jsx); they can either:
  - Claim an unclaimed player slot in the group, or
  - Join as a brand-new player.

## 2. Game share links (public)
- Format: `https://squadupv2.web.app/game-invite/{gameId}`.
- Recipient lands on [GameInvitePage.jsx](../src/pages/GameInvitePage.jsx) without needing a SquadUp account — `signInAnonymously` is used for read access.
- Guests can swipe IN as guests; their response is deduplicated via `localStorage`.
- Anonymous accounts are reaped after 24h by the `cleanupAnonymousUsers` Cloud Function.

For the original detailed walkthrough, see [../GAME_INVITE_FLOW.md](../GAME_INVITE_FLOW.md).
